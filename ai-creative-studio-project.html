PROJECT CASE STUDY

AI Creative Studio

An AI-powered creative web experience designed to transform ideas into professional design concepts and detailed AI image prompts.

Project Overview

AI Creative Studio is an interactive creative tool that combines artificial intelligence, graphic design, UI/UX thinking and front-end development into one modern digital experience.


The goal was to create a simple interface where users can enter a creative idea and receive a structured professional concept that can be used for design projects.

DESIGN PROCESS

From Idea to Product

The project followed a simple creative workflow designed to reduce complexity for the user.

1

Understand the Idea

The user enters a creative idea along with optional information such as style, audience, colours or purpose.

2

Generate Concept

The AI processes the input and creates a structured professional creative direction.

3

Improve & Regenerate

Users can improve or regenerate the concept to explore different creative directions.

4

Create Image Prompt

The generated concept can be transformed into a detailed AI image-generation prompt.

KEY FEATURES

Built for Creative Workflows
